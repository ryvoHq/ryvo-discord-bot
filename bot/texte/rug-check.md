# Rug check: what to look at before you buy
Post the contract address (CA) and the chain. Others help you check. This is a learning channel, not a buy list.

**On-chain**
· **Mint authority** revoked? If not, the dev can print new tokens.
· **Freeze authority** revoked? If not, the dev can freeze your tokens (Solana).
· **Liquidity** burned or locked, and how much is in the pool?
· **Top 10 holders:** how much do they hold together, and is the dev wallet among them?
· **Bundled launch:** did a group of fresh wallets buy in the first block?
· **Dev wallet history:** did this wallet launch coins before, and what happened to them?
· **Sell test:** can holders actually sell, or is it a honeypot?

**Off-chain**
· Socials: how old is the X account, are followers real, is the website more than a template?
· Who is talking about it: organic accounts, or paid "callers" all posting at once?

Even a clean check does not make a coin safe. Most new coins go to zero. Never risk money you need.
