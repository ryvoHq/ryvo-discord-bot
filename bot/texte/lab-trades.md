# RYVO Lab
The Lab runs a fixed, rules-based trading model on paper and posts every trade here: entry, stop, target and the result in R. Wins and losses stay visible, so everyone can learn what works and what doesn't.

**The rules (fixed on 25 Nov 2026, not changed afterwards)**
· Checked once a day after the daily close, on large crypto, the biggest memecoins, indices, gold, silver, oil, EUR/USD, NVDA and TSLA.
· **Long:** daily close above the prior 20-day high and above the 50-day average.
· **Short:** daily close below the prior 20-day low and below the 50-day average.
· **Stop** 1.5× the 14-day ATR, **target** 3× ATR (2R). Time exit after 15 days. One trade per market at a time. 0.1% costs per trade are deducted.

**Important:** these are simulated trades. No real money is involved, and this is not financial advice or a signal service. Most simple breakout rules lose more often than they win. That is exactly what the Lab is here to show.
