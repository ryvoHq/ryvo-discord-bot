# RYVO Lab
Paper trades from a fixed rule set, posted live. Every trade stays in the log, losers included.

Long: daily close above the prior 20-day high and above the 50-day average. Short: the mirror image. Stop 1.5× ATR(14), target 3× ATR (2R), max 15 days, 0.1% costs per trade. Rules fixed on 25 Nov 2026.

Simulated, no real money, not financial advice.
