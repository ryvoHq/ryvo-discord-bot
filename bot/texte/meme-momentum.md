# Meme momentum
An alert appears here when one of the large memecoins (DOGE, PEPE, BONK, WIF, POPCAT, FARTCOIN, PENGU, TRUMP, MOODENG, BRETT, SPX6900, SHIB) moves 6% or more within the hour. Each coin alerts at most once every 3 hours.

New launches are not covered here. Use {kanal:rug-check} before you touch anything new. Alerts describe a move. They are not calls.
