# FAQ
**What is RYVO?**
A trading community and the team behind the RYVO Terminal, a tool that brings market data, orderflow and news together in one screen.

**Is this a signal group?**
No. There are no paid signals and no copy trading here. You get data, news, a descriptive market brief and a community to discuss ideas.

**What is the RYVO Lab?**
A fixed, rules-based model that trades on paper and posts every trade, win or loss, in {kanal:lab-trades}. It exists to learn in public. The trades are simulated and are not recommendations.

**Is it free?**
Yes. The community, market news and the market brief are free. **RYVO Pro** will add deeper data and real-time alerts. See {kanal:pro-info}.

**How do I get language channels?**
Open {customize} and choose your languages. You can change it any time.

**Where can I try the RYVO Terminal?**
Watch {kanal:terminal-updates}. Beta access and the waitlist are announced there.

**Someone DMed me offering signals or help with my account.**
That is a scam. Staff never DM first. Block them and report them in {kanal:support}.

**How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?**
{kanal:bug-reports} and {kanal:feature-requests}.
