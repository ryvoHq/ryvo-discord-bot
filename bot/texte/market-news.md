# Market news
Headlines are collected automatically from official agencies (Federal Reserve, SEC, CFTC, ECB and others) and a curated list of established outlets. Each post shows the title, the source and a link. Open the source to read the full story.

Topics: crypto, rates and central banks, regulation, inflation, labour market, oil.
Headlines can arrive with a delay. This channel describes what is being reported. It does not tell you what to trade.
