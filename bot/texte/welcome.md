# Welcome to RYVO
RYVO is an international trading community and the home of the **RYVO Terminal**: market data, orderflow and news in one place.

Here you get market headlines as they land, a descriptive market brief twice a day, and a place to talk memecoins, swing and day trading with people who take it seriously.

# Stay safe
RYVO staff will **never** DM you first, never ask for money, passwords, seed phrases or private keys, and never manage your account. Anyone who does is a scammer. Report them in {kanal:support}.
