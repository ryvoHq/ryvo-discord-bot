# Market sessions
Times in UTC. The US switches to daylight saving time from mid-March to early November, Europe from late March to late October. In those weeks, US and European times are one hour earlier in UTC.

```
Session            Winter (UTC)    Summer (UTC)
Sydney             22:00 – 07:00   21:00 – 06:00
Tokyo              00:00 – 09:00   00:00 – 09:00
London             08:00 – 16:30   07:00 – 15:30
New York stocks    14:30 – 21:00   13:30 – 20:00
US futures (CME)   23:00 – 22:00   22:00 – 21:00
Crypto             24/7            24/7
```
Most US economic data comes out at 08:30 New York time (13:30 UTC in winter, 12:30 UTC in summer). Rate decisions are at 14:00 New York time.
