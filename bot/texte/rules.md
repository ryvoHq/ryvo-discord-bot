# Server rules
**1. Respect.** No harassment, hate, slurs or personal attacks. Disagree with ideas, not people.
**2. 18+.** Trading content is for adults. If you are under 18, this server is not for you.
**3. No financial advice, no signals for sale.** Share ideas and analysis, not instructions. Nobody here tells you what to buy.
**4. Disclose your position.** If you post a call or idea on something you hold, say so.
**5. No pump coordination.** Organising buys or sells to move a price is market manipulation and gets you banned.
**6. No shilling, no paid promotion.** No undisclosed sponsored posts, no referral or affiliate links, no invites to other servers or groups.
**7. No DM solicitation.** Offering signals, "account management" or "fund recovery" in DMs is a scam. Instant ban.
**8. Never share secrets.** No seed phrases, private keys, passwords or screenshots that show them.
**9. Right channel, right language.** English in the main channels. Other languages in their own channels (pick them in {customize}).
**10. Honest results.** Post losses as well as wins. Fake PnL screenshots get removed.
**11. Follow Discord's rules.** [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

Moderators act on the spirit of these rules, not only the wording. Breaking them leads to a warning, a timeout or a ban.
