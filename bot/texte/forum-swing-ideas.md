# How to post a swing idea
**Title:** `TICKER · Long/Short · timeframe`, for example `ETH · Long · 2–4 weeks`

**In the post:**
· Chart with your levels marked
· Entry zone
· Invalidation: where you are wrong (your stop)
· Targets
· Why: structure, levels, catalyst
· Position: do you hold it already?

**Tags:** pick Long or Short and the market. When the idea is done, add **Closed: Win** or **Closed: Loss** and write one line on what happened.

Ideas are members' opinions, not advice. RYVO does not post signals.
