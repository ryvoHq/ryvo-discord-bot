# The risks are real
**Trading can lose you all of your money.** Leveraged products (futures, perps, CFDs, options) can lose more than you put in.
**Memecoins are extremely high risk.** Most new coins go to zero. Rug pulls, honeypots and insider dumps are common.
**Past results do not predict future results.** Screenshots, win rates and backtests, including our own, do not show what will happen next.
**Only trade money you can afford to lose.**

# About the RYVO Lab
Trades in the Lab channels are simulated paper trades from a fixed rules-based model. No real money is traded, past Lab results say nothing about future results, and nothing there is a recommendation to buy or sell. RYVO holds no positions because of Lab trades.
