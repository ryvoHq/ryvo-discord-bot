# Swing board
Every evening after the US close the bot posts a snapshot for swing traders: weekly and monthly change, distance to the 50- and 200-day average and the position in the 20-day range, plus a 6-month chart.

It describes where markets stand. It does not say where they will go. Discuss setups in {kanal:swing-chat} and post your ideas in {kanal:swing-ideas}.
