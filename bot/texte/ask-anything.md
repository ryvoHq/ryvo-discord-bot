# Quick glossary
**Long / short:** betting on a rise / a fall.
**Stop-loss:** the price where you exit because the idea was wrong.
**Take-profit (TP):** the price where you lock in gains.
**R:R (risk-reward):** possible gain divided by possible loss. 3R = the target is three times the distance of the stop.
**Leverage:** trading a bigger position than your money. 10× leverage means a 10% move against you wipes the position.
**Liquidation:** the exchange closes your leveraged position because the margin is gone.
**Funding rate:** the fee longs and shorts pay each other on perpetual futures to keep the price close to spot.
**Open interest (OI):** the total size of open futures positions.
**Perp:** perpetual future, a futures contract without an expiry date.
**Market cap (MC):** price × circulating supply.
**Liquidity (LP):** money in the trading pool. Low liquidity means big price jumps and hard exits.
**FVG (fair value gap):** a price area that was skipped in a fast move.
**Support / resistance:** price zones where buying or selling showed up before.
**Moving average (50D, 200D):** the average close of the last 50 or 200 days.
**Drawdown:** how far an account fell from its high.
